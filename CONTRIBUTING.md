# Contributing to kizu-lang/bench

Thank you for your interest in `kizu-lang/bench`.

This repository is maintained solely by the author, and pull request and issue
creation is restricted to maintainers on GitHub.

## Feedback, bug reports, and ideas

Please share questions, bug reports, benchmark methodology feedback, and ideas
in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/kizu-lang/bench/discussions). The
author reviews posts there and decides what to address directly.
